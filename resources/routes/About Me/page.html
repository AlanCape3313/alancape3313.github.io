Overview

As a developer, I mastered my skills over time, as I've always enjoyed creating in-game objects. I started like any kid, building, then creating basic maps with command blocks and redstone, until one day I entered the world of addons, and it was wonderful. I never left that world.

I'm passionate about creating anything, but I'm especially passionate about creating bossfights. I always created scenarios in my head of some epic fight to make them a reality in the game. It's what I enjoy creating the most, so entity development is what I'm most proficient at.

I love challenges. I love creating things that everyone thinks aren't possible in-game and then showing them off everywhere 😂. But generally, I'm passionate about the whole creation thing.
